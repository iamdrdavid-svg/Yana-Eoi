### 3.3 Affordable housing — RED
- **Held:** 3% of residential GFA in perpetuity (locked), about 866 m², about 7 homes.
- **Missing:** the dwelling count, a named CHP, form and ownership.
- **Tested against the criterion (R-a2 to R-a4, LOCATED):**

  - **Proportionality: not met on the comparators.** The uplift sought is the largest in the centre's located record: 16 m to 133.9 m, about 14:1 on a sub-minimum lot. Yet the offer is the lowest:

    | Comparator | Scheme | Offer | Outcome |

    | 465 Victoria Ave, Chatswood (R-d1) | 44 storeys | 10% of dwellings perpetual | declared 15 Sep 2026 |
    | HDA-recommended schemes (R-b3) | — | 15% of GFA perpetual | recommended |
    | 16-20 Old Castle Hill Rd (R-c1) | 40 storeys | 15% for 15 years | declared (per D2) |
    | 15-17 Ashford Ave (R-c3) | 15 storeys | 10% for 15 years | not recommended (reasons not read) |

  - **Policy baseline:** the in-fill bonus alone requires 10–15% (for 15 years) for +30% height. YANA's 3% buys +737%.
- **Objection:** "Seven perpetual homes for a 737% height increase is not proportional. It is below every comparator I have, including a much smaller uplift two blocks away."
- **Fix:** none within the locked position. See OD-8 (variants A and B, drafted in webform.md). Strengthen what can be strengthened inside 3%: name the CHP, state the dwelling count, state the form (built, pepper-potted), and secure it in the LEP.

**Basis.** The HDA has recommended about half the EOIs it considered (R-a1, LOCATED). Castle Hill has at least one declared 40-storey concurrent rezoning (R-c1), so height alone is not disqualifying. Against that, the criteria now expressly tie the affordable offer to the size of the uplift (R-a2). YANA's uplift is the largest in the located record and its offer the smallest: below a 5% perpetual offer on a far smaller uplift in the same centre (R-c5), and below the 10% perpetual offer declared at Chatswood on 15 Sep 2026 (R-d1). Hills EOIs have failed on strategic merit (R-b4). A 2,048 m² site that also needs cl 4.1A relief adds an isolation objection. Each of these is a stated ground the HDA has used.

These estimates rest on search summaries, not primary records. They should be re-based once the HDA records and the live form are read (CA-0).
