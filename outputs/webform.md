**Q48. Summary of the rezoning proposal and its strategic merit**
The rezoning amends the height of buildings map and the minimum lot size standard for one consolidated R4 site, to allow a 243-home residential tower 623 m from Castle Hill Metro.

Strategic merit:
- **Changed circumstances.** The site's 16 m height control predates the Sydney Plan, which commenced on 13 August 2026 and replaced the region and district plans the local controls were written under. The Hills Shire must deliver 23,300 homes by 2029 and is required to boost its housing capacity.
- **Capacity that will be built.** The Feasible Housing Capacity Policy separates zoned capacity from capacity that is realisable. This site is consolidated, serviced, with a defined scheme, and needs no amalgamation.
- **Context.** Castle Hill's high-density zone is already changing through State-assessed projects: 16–20 Old Castle Hill Road (40 storeys, 371 homes, SSD-85238209); 93–107 Cecil Avenue and 9–10 Roger Avenue, on the same street (610 apartments in 23 to 35 storey buildings, SSD-78156221); and 244–248 Old Northern Road, on the same block (up to 200 apartments, SSD-106965706).
- **No other State-assessed pathway.** See Q50.
> Source notes: R-h4, R-k1, R-k5, R-k6, R-j5, R-j6; SSD-78156221 description is from the owner's reading of the Portal page (Sep 2026), not re-read. **Red-team note: this answer does not overcome the two June 2026 refusals on Cecil Avenue or the LMR point. **Q50. Details of changes, including whether additional to existing bonuses**
The changes are sought instead of, not on top of, the in-fill affordable housing bonus. That bonus adds 30% to the height and floor space otherwise available. On this site the base is the Low and Mid-Rise housing standard of `[planner to confirm: 17.5 m and 1.5:1 (outer area) or 22 m and 2.2:1 (inner area)]`. With the bonus that is 22.75 m and 1.95:1, about 34 homes, or at most 28.6 m and 2.86:1, about 49 homes. Either result is below the 100-home threshold for State significance. No other State-assessed pathway can deliver this housing on this site.
